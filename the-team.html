Meet the Team

Our Team

SWAG is led by UC Davis Division 1 water polo athletes who volunteer their time to make water safety accessible to everyone.

Sadie

Co-Founder

“I am passionate about SWAG because water safety is often overlooked, yet it is life-saving. By offering free survival swimming in Davis, we’re creating a safe, supportive, and empowering space where professors, staff, and students can feel truly confident around the water.”

Lillie

Co-Founder

“I want every Aggie to feel confident and safe in the water. Water safety isn’t just a skill — it’s peace of mind, freedom, and confidence that carries far beyond the pool.”
