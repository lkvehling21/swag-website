Our Why

The case for water safety

3x

Adults who took structured survival swimming sessions have a higher chance of self-rescue in unexpected immersion.

88%

Taking a swim session reduces the risk of drowning by 88%.

2x

People with basic water training survived twice as long in open water compared to non-swimmers.

Why we do this

Sadie

Co-Founder

“I am passionate about SWAG because water safety is often overlooked, yet it is life-saving. By offering free survival swimming at UC Davis, we’re creating a safe, supportive, and empowering space where professors, staff, and students can feel truly confident around the water.”

Lillie

Co-Founder

“I want every Aggie to feel confident and safe in the water. Water safety isn’t just a skill, it’s peace of mind, freedom, and confidence that carries far beyond the pool.”
