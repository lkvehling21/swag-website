Making Waves for Safety: UC Davis Athletes Launch Free Swim Initiative

UC Davis Division I water polo players Lillie Vehling and Sadie Henry launched SWAG, a free water safety program for the campus community. "Water safety isn't just a want, it's a need," says Henry, as the pair work to close a widespread gap in adult swim education.
